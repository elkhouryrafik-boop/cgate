Aldermere Development Foundation — Example General Conditions, Part 7: Integrity

7.1 Standards of conduct. The Contractor shall observe the highest standard of ethics throughout the procurement process and the performance of the Contract. The Contractor shall not engage in corrupt, fraudulent, coercive, collusive or obstructive conduct in connection with the Contract, and shall ensure that its Personnel and subcontractors do not do so either.

7.2 Gifts and hospitality. The Contractor warrants that no official of the Purchaser has received from or on behalf of the Contractor any gifts, favours or hospitality, and that no staff member of the Purchaser has been offered employment or any other benefit in connection with the award of the Contract. The Contractor shall not offer any such benefit during the term of the Contract.

7.3 Former officials. During the two (2) year period after an official has left the service of the Purchaser, the Contractor shall not engage that former official in any capacity connected with the Contract without the prior written consent of the Purchaser.

7.4 Conflicts of interest. The Contractor shall disclose to the Purchaser any actual or potential conflict of interest, or any situation that could reasonably be perceived as a conflict of interest, as soon as it becomes aware of it, and shall take the steps the Purchaser requires to resolve it.

7.5 Eligibility. The Contractor represents that neither it nor any of its Affiliates has been debarred, and that it is not subject to any suspension or exclusion imposed by a development financing institution that is recognised by the Purchaser. The Contractor shall immediately notify the Purchaser if it or any of its Affiliates becomes subject to any such measure.

7.6 Independent bidding. The Contractor confirms that it prepared its proposal independently and without consultation, communication or arrangement with any other bidder or competitor, and that it has not submitted more than one proposal for the Contract.

7.7 Misrepresentation. The Contractor shall not make any false statement or material omission to the Purchaser. All information supplied in the proposal and during performance must be accurate and complete, and any misrepresentation entitles the Purchaser to terminate the Contract immediately.

7.8 Subcontracting. The Contractor shall not subcontract any part of the services without the prior written approval of the Purchaser. The Contractor remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors and shall flow down the requirements of this Part to each of them.

7.9 Consequences. Where the Contractor breaches this Part, the Purchaser may disqualify it from current and future procurements, may terminate the Contract without any liability to the Contractor, and may recover any sums paid in connection with the breach.
