Aldermere Development Foundation — Example General Conditions, Part 9: Conduct, Records and Cooperation

9.1 Code of Conduct. The Contractor shall comply with the Foundation Supplier Code of Conduct, a copy of which has been provided with the solicitation, and with the policies applicable to its work on premises of the Purchaser. The Contractor represents that it has reviewed the Code of Conduct and its Personnel have been informed of it.

9.2 Safeguarding. The Contractor shall take all appropriate measures to prevent sexual exploitation and abuse, and harassment of any kind, by its Personnel, and shall comply with the safeguarding standards of the Purchaser. The Contractor shall not employ any person in forced labour or in the worst forms of child labour.

9.3 Reporting. The Contractor shall report any allegation of misconduct involving its Personnel to the Purchaser within five (5) working days of becoming aware of it, and shall cooperate fully with any investigation that the Purchaser carries out.

9.4 Investigations. The Contractor shall cooperate with any audit or investigation carried out by or for the Purchaser and shall give investigators access to its relevant records, premises and Personnel.

9.5 Records. The Contractor shall retain all records relating to the Contract for a period of seven (7) years after final payment, and shall produce them to the Purchaser on request.

9.6 Confidentiality. Each party shall treat the confidential information of the other as confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party, except where disclosure is required by law. The Contractor shall process any personal data supplied by the Purchaser only in accordance with the written instructions of the Purchaser and applicable data protection law.

9.7 Insurance. The Contractor shall maintain professional liability insurance and public liability insurance for the duration of the Contract, in the amounts stated in the Special Conditions, and shall give the Purchaser a copy of each policy on request.

9.8 Invoicing. Invoices shall be submitted monthly in a form agreed with the Purchaser. The Purchaser will pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days of receipt. Delay in payment does not entitle the Contractor to suspend the services.
